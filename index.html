The Art of Multi-Instrumentalism

Originally from Ukraine and now based in Dubai, I am a multi-instrumental musician proficient in a diverse array of instruments including classical guitar, piano, acoustic guitar, electric guitar, bass guitar, ukulele, and Ukrainian folk instruments.

With formal music education and almost 20 years of dedication, my passion lies in teaching children and adults the profound language of music. My mastery of different musical styles—ranging from Baroque and classical to jazz, rock, and pop—enables me to connect with students from all cultural traditions.

Contribution to the UAE's Cultural Landscape

"As a music teacher and performer in Dubai, I have actively contributed to the cultural enrichment of the city.
